# AI Policy

Using AI tools (LLMs) to help write code for this project is fine. The bar for
every contribution is the same, whoever or whatever wrote it.

You are responsible for every line you submit. If you can't explain a change in
your own words, don't open the pull request. Read the diff and understand it
before you ask anyone else to.

The maintainer is accountable for everything that ships in a release. That is
why nothing gets merged on the strength of "the AI wrote it".

Write your descriptions and replies yourself. Don't paste AI output in answer
to review questions. If you want to quote AI output for context, put it in a
quote block, label it, and add your own commentary on why it matters.

A human has to be in the loop. Autonomous agents opening issues or pull
requests are not welcome, and those contributions will be closed.

Using AI to polish your English or to translate is fine. Make sure the result
still sounds like you and says what you mean.
